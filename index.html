The magic of not seeing the photo.

No preview and no retakes: every shot uploads the moment it’s taken. Each guest gets a set number of shots, so every frame counts.

Good questions.

Anything else, write to allshots.app@gmail.com.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code or type the 6-character code, and a page opens in their phone’s browser. Tapping Photo opens the phone’s own camera. Guests who have the iPhone app can use its camera instead, which is where disposable mode works in full, and everything lands in the same album.

Does it work on Android?

For guests, yes: any modern phone browser works. To create and manage an event you need an iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, or a computer, at host.allshots.app.

What is disposable mode?

A camera with a set number of shots and no preview or retakes: every shot uploads the moment it’s taken. It’s on by default in the iPhone app. You choose 10 to 72 shots per guest, or unlimited shots on the Unlimited plan.

Can guests see each other’s photos?

You choose. In a shared album, everyone who joins can browse every photo as it arrives. In a private album, guests keep shooting and only you see the whole album.

Who can open the album?

It isn’t public or indexed by search engines. Anyone who joins with your QR code or code can open it, so share the code the way you’d share the invite.

Is it a subscription?

No. Event packages are one-time purchases for one event, and every event starts free for up to 10 guests. If you host often, monthly and annual plans are also in the app.

What if my guest list grows?

Upgrade the same event from the app at any time.

Can guests record video?

Yes, on the Unlimited plan, up to 90 seconds per clip.

Are photos saved in full resolution?

No. Photos are resized on the phone before upload, to 1920 px wide in the app and 1600 px on the long edge in the browser, so they upload quickly on venue Wi-Fi. Re-encoding them on the phone also removes their location data. Videos aren’t re-encoded.

How long are photos kept?

On the free Starter plan, 7 days after the first upload, and you get a notification the day before. Paid events aren’t deleted automatically: the album stays until you delete the event. Guests can open a paid album for 30 days after the event date, or after the first photo if the event has no date.

Can I remove a photo?

Yes. You can delete any photo from the album, and anything a guest reports is hidden at once while it’s reviewed.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something went wrong on our side, write to us first. See the refund policy.
