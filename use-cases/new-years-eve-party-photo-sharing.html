New Year’s Eve party photo sharing: every phone, one album

At midnight, every phone in the room goes up. By morning those photos are spread across everyone’s camera rolls. One QR code puts them all in one album you can download over breakfast.

By the AllShots team · Updated November 2026

Set it up before the countdown

Create the event in the afternoon, print the QR code on one of the 12 invitation and table-card designs, and put it by the drinks. Guests scan it and a page opens in their phone’s browser, on iPhone or Android: nothing to install, no account. Drop the link in the group chat too, so people who arrive late can join in seconds. Anyone who can’t scan can type the 6-character code at allshots.app/app.

The midnight shot list

  • The last photo of the year: the whole room, five minutes before.
  • The countdown, from every corner at once.
  • The first toast of the new year.
  • The midnight kiss, or the midnight hug.
  • The first group photo of the year, at 12:01.
  • The aftermath: confetti on the floor, shoes by the door.

Want the countdown on video?

Video is on the Unlimited plan ($29.99): guests can record clips of up to 90 seconds, enough for the countdown and the cheering after it. On the other plans, guests share photos.

Resolutions, in their own words

On Medium and Unlimited, each guest can leave one written note of up to 240 characters and one voice message of up to 60 seconds. Ask everyone for a resolution or a wish for the year; you download the notes as a PDF and the voice messages as one ZIP, ready to read back next December.

Make every shot count

Disposable mode gives each guest a set number of shots: 10, 12, 24, 36, 48 or 72, or unlimited on the Unlimited plan. In the iPhone app there’s no preview and no retake; guests in the browser use their phone’s own camera, with the same shot count. Give everyone 24 for the night and watch them save a few for midnight.

The morning after

Every photo is already in one album. Download it as one ZIP, on any plan. On the free Starter plan, photos are deleted 7 days after the first upload, with a notification the day before. Paid events aren’t deleted automatically, and guests can open a paid album for 30 days after the event date.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event in the iPhone app or at host.allshots.app. Name it, add the date and choose how many shots each guest gets. No account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it, put it by the drinks, drop the link in the group chat.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. A page opens in their browser: nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live album you control: delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

Can guests record the countdown on video?

Yes, on the Unlimited plan: clips of up to 90 seconds. The other plans are for photos.

Do guests need the app?

No. They scan the QR code and a page opens in the phone’s browser, on iPhone or Android. There’s no account, and they can add a name or stay anonymous.

How long can guests see the album after the party?

On a paid plan, guests can open the album for 30 days after the event date, and the album stays for you until you delete the event. On the free Starter plan, photos are deleted 7 days after the first upload.

Is it free?

For up to 10 guests and 50 photos in total, yes. Bigger parties pay once: $3.99, $14.99 or $29.99 per event, with no subscription.
