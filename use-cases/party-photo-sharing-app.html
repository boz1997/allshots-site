Parties

A photo sharing app for any party

Dinner parties, house parties, engagements, retirements, reunions, a day on a boat. Different parties, same problem: the photos are on everyone else’s phone.

By the AllShots team · Updated 23 September 2026

How it works at a party

Stick the QR on the fridge, stand it on the table or drop the link in the group chat. Guests scan, the camera opens in their browser, and every photo goes straight into one live album. Nobody downloads anything, nobody makes an account, and nobody has to ask “can you send me that one?” the next morning.

Pick the feel

  • Disposable: every guest gets a fixed number of shots (10 to 72). In the iPhone app there is no preview and no retake, so people stop posing and start shooting.
  • Gallery & preview: guests can check a shot before sharing it and add photos from their camera roll.
  • Shared or private album: everyone sees everything, or each guest sees only their own photos while you see all of them.

Parties it works for

Engagement parties, dinner parties, house-warmings, retirements, reunions, holiday parties and yacht days. If people bring phones, it works. For birthdays and baby showers we have dedicated pages: birthday parties and baby showers.

You stay in charge

You can delete any photo, anything a guest reports is hidden immediately, and ending the event stops new uploads and joins. When the party is over, download the whole album as a ZIP.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event in the iPhone app. Name it, add the date and choose how many shots each guest gets. No account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it on a sign, put it on the tables, drop the link in the group chat.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. The camera opens in their browser: nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live album you control: delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code and the camera opens in their browser. iPhone users can also use the AllShots app.

Is it free?

Starter is free for up to 10 guests and 50 photos in total. The photos are deleted 7 days after the first upload, so download the ZIP that week.

Can I keep guests from seeing each other’s photos?

Yes. Use a private album: each guest sees only the photos they took, and you see all of them.

Can I download everything at the end?

Yes, the whole album as one ZIP, on every plan.
