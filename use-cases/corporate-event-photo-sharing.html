Company events

Photo sharing for corporate events and team offsites

The official photographer gets the keynote. Your colleagues get the karaoke. Here is how to collect the second kind into an album the organiser controls — without asking anyone to install anything on a work phone.

By the AllShots team · Updated 21 September 2026

Where it fits

  • Holiday parties and summer events. The photos people want are the ones of each other, not of the stage.
  • Offsites and team days. Three days, five locations, forty phones. One album.
  • Conferences and launches. A QR code on the badge or the lanyard; attendees add their photos, the marketing team downloads the lot the next morning.
  • Internal comms. The album link goes into the wrap-up email instead of a Google Drive folder nobody opens.

Why it works on work phones

Nothing is installed. An attendee scans the QR code and the camera opens in their browser; no account, no company login, no app-store approval. The iPhone app exists for hosts who want it, but attendees never need it. That is the difference between 20 percent participation and 70.

Control for the organiser

  • Hide or delete any photo from the album, instantly.
  • Private mode: attendees see only their own uploads; the organiser sees everything. Right for events where colleagues would rather not browse each other’s photos, or where the album will be curated before it is shared.
  • Reveal timing: show photos live, or hold everything until the next day so the album goes out reviewed.
  • End the event to stop new uploads; download the ZIP for the comms team.

Privacy, plainly

Taking part should be genuinely optional for employees, and nobody should feel they have to be photographed. Put a line on the sign and in the invitation — our host guide has wording — and check whether your organisation wants its works council or data protection officer involved. Organisations that need a data processing agreement with us can write to allshots.app@gmail.com; our privacy policy describes where photos are stored and for how long.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event on host.allshots.app or in the iPhone app. Name, date, how many shots each guest gets — done in about sixty seconds, no account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it on a sign, put it on the tables, drop the link in the group chat.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. The camera opens in their browser — nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live gallery you control: hide or delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

Do attendees need to install an app on their work phone?

No. The QR code opens the camera in the phone’s browser. No app, no account, nothing for IT to approve.

Can the organiser review photos before anyone sees them?

Yes. Set the reveal timing to next day or make the album private; the organiser sees everything live and can hide or delete photos before the album is shared.

Is there per-seat or per-employee pricing?

No. One payment covers one event. Unlimited ($29.99) has no cap on attendees.

Do you provide a data processing agreement?

Yes, on request — write to allshots.app@gmail.com. Our privacy policy explains where photos are stored and how long they are kept.
