Homecoming

Photo sharing for homecoming

Homecoming is a weekend, not a night: the parade, the game, the dance, the alumni tailgate. Give it one album and one code.

By the AllShots team · Updated 23 September 2026

One code for the whole weekend

Create one event, call it something like “Homecoming 2026”, and use the same QR everywhere: the programme, the parade float, the stadium gate, the dance entrance. The album stays open until you end it, so guests who join on Friday can keep adding on Saturday. For a whole weekend, pick a generous number of shots per guest (up to 72), or unlimited shots on the Unlimited plan.

Alumni and reunion classes

Reunion classes often meet at homecoming. Put the code in the alumni newsletter and the reunion group chat. If you choose Gallery & preview, alumni can add photos from their camera roll too, including the ones they took on the way in. On Medium and Unlimited every guest can also leave one written note (up to 240 characters) and one voice message (up to 60 seconds): the reunion’s guest book, without the book.

The student dance

The dance follows the same rules as prom: check the school’s photo policy, tell guests photos are being collected, and consider a private album so each student sees only their own photos while the organisers see everything. You can delete any photo, and reported photos are hidden immediately. AllShots is not directed to children under 16; younger students should use it only with a parent’s or guardian’s consent.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event on host.allshots.app or in the iPhone app. Name, date, how many shots each guest gets — done in about sixty seconds, no account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it on a sign, put it on the tables, drop the link in the group chat.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. The camera opens in their browser — nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live gallery you control: hide or delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

Can guests join at different times over the weekend?

Yes. The album is live and stays open until you end the event. Guests join once and keep adding photos until they reach their shot limit.

Can alumni who were not there add old photos?

If you choose Gallery & preview when you create the event, anyone with the code can upload from their camera roll.

How do we get all the photos at the end?

Download the whole album as a ZIP. That works on every plan, including the free one.

Do guests need an app?

No. The camera opens in the browser. Anyone who cannot scan can type the 6-character code at allshots.app/app.
