Christmas party photo sharing: one QR code, every guest’s photos

The ugly-sweater lineup, the gift swap, the toast at the long table. Everyone at your Christmas party takes photos, and almost none of them reach you. Put one QR code by the tree and they all land in one album.

By the AllShots team · Updated November 2026

How it works at a Christmas party

Put the QR code where people stop: by the tree, on the bar, on the fridge. Guests scan it and a page opens in the phone’s browser, on iPhone or Android. They tap Photo, their phone’s camera opens, and every photo lands in one live album. Nothing to install, no account, no password, and guests can add a name or stay anonymous.

Anyone who can’t scan a code can type the 6-character event code at allshots.app/app instead.

A shot list for the night

  • The ugly-sweater lineup, before anyone takes theirs off.
  • The gift swap: the steal, the regift, the face when it opens.
  • The cookie table, before and after.
  • The kids at the window, if their parents are happy with it (more on that below).
  • The toast, from three different seats.
  • The group photo by the tree, taken by whoever has the longest arms.
  • The last guests still at the table at midnight.

Make it a disposable camera

Turn on disposable mode and each guest gets a set number of shots: 10, 12, 24, 36, 48 or 72, or unlimited on the Unlimited plan. In the iPhone app there’s no preview and no retake; guests in the browser use their phone’s own camera, with the same shot count. A limit makes people save a few shots for the moments that count.

Shared album or private album

In a shared album, everyone who joins can browse every photo as it arrives, which is half the fun on the night. In a private album, guests keep shooting and only you see the whole album. If children will be in the photos, our host guide suggests asking their parents first, and a private album keeps every photo with you.

Holiday messages, too

On Medium and Unlimited, each guest can leave one written note of up to 240 characters and one voice message of up to 60 seconds, right from the browser. A toast from someone who had to leave early, a greeting from a grandparent: you download the notes as a PDF and the voice messages as one ZIP.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event in the iPhone app or at host.allshots.app. Name it, add the date and choose how many shots each guest gets. No account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it on one of the 12 card designs, stand it by the tree, drop the link in the group chat.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. A page opens in their browser: nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live album you control: delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code and a page opens in the phone’s browser, on iPhone or Android. Guests with the AllShots iPhone app can use it instead, and everything lands in the same album.

Is it free for a small family Christmas?

Yes, for up to 10 guests. Starter holds 50 photos in total, and they are deleted 7 days after the first upload, so download the ZIP that week.

Can guests share videos?

Video is on the Unlimited plan, with clips of up to 90 seconds. The other plans are for photos, and Medium adds written notes and voice messages.

How do I get all the photos after the party?

Download the whole album as one ZIP, on every plan, free included.
