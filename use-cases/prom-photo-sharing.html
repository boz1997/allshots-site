Photo sharing for prom

Everyone at prom is taking photos, and by midnight they are scattered across a hundred phones. One album catches them.

By the AllShots team · Updated 23 September 2026

Pre-prom photos

The front-yard and park photos before the dance are the ones families care about most, and the parents are the photographers. Drop the album link in the parents’ group chat or print the QR on the invitation to the pre-prom gathering. Parents scan, shoot, and their photos land in the same album as the dance.

For the prom committee

One album for the whole dance: the QR on a sign at the entrance and on the tables, the code in the student announcement. You stay in control of it.

  • Delete anything. The host can remove any photo, file and all.
  • Reports act at once. A photo a guest reports is hidden immediately.
  • Private album. Each student sees only the photos they took, while the committee sees everything.
  • End the event. Ending it stops uploads and new joins. It cannot be undone, so do it when the night is over.

Disposable-camera mode

Give every guest a fixed number of shots: 10, 12, 24, 36, 48 or 72. In the iPhone app, disposable mode uploads each shot instantly with no preview and no retake, which is exactly what makes people take the candid photos. On the web camera the shot limit still applies, but the phone’s own camera may offer a retake.

School rules and ages

Check your school’s photo policy before you put a code on the wall, and tell guests that photos are being collected. Our host guide has wording for the sign. AllShots is not directed to children under 16; younger students should use it only with a parent’s or guardian’s consent.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event on host.allshots.app or in the iPhone app. Name, date, how many shots each guest gets — done in about sixty seconds, no account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it on a sign, put it on the tables, drop the link in the group chat.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. The camera opens in their browser — nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live gallery you control: hide or delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

Can the committee control what ends up in the album?

Yes. The host can delete any photo, reported photos are hidden immediately, and ending the event stops all uploads and new joins.

Can students upload videos?

On the Unlimited plan, yes, up to 90 seconds each.

Do students need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code and the camera opens in their browser. There is no account to create.

Can one album cover pre-prom, the dance and the after-party?

Yes. One event, one code, and it stays open until you end it.
