GuestPix Alternative: AllShots vs GuestPix (2026)

GuestPix is a full wedding gallery in the browser: original full-resolution downloads, a live slideshow, a guestbook and a year of hosting. AllShots is a simpler live album that starts free and tops out at $29.99 per event. Here is how they compare, from GuestPix’s own pages.

By the AllShots team · Updated November 2026

AllShots is our app. Everything about GuestPix on this page comes from its wedding pricing page and FAQ, checked on November 13, 2026. GuestPix loads its package prices with a script and a currency switcher, so we only quote the “from $49” on its page; check its pricing page for each package.

The short answer

  • Choose GuestPix if you want original full-resolution files, a live slideshow, a written and video guestbook, an invitation with RSVPs, long upload and hosting windows, or a 14-day money back guarantee.
  • Choose AllShots if the event or the budget is small: free for up to 10 guests, $3.99 for up to 20, $14.99 for up to 50 and $29.99 for any number. You also get disposable mode, voice messages from guests, and both an iPhone app and a web dashboard for the host.

When GuestPix is the better pick

  • You need originals. GuestPix’s ZIP has photos and videos in original full resolution; AllShots resizes photos on the phone.
  • You want a slideshow at the reception, or a video guestbook.
  • You want long windows. Guests can keep uploading for 12 months on Signature, and galleries are hosted for 12 months from the event date.
  • You have several events. The Signature Bundle has three separate galleries.
  • Guests speak different languages. The gallery is multi-lingual: EN, FR, ES, DE, IT, JP and 5 more.

When AllShots is the better pick

  • A small event or a small budget. Free for up to 10 guests, $3.99 for up to 20, $14.99 for up to 50 and $29.99 with no guest limit, all one time per event.
  • You like the disposable-camera idea. Disposable mode gives each guest 10 to 72 shots, or unlimited on Unlimited.
  • You want to hear your guests. Voice messages of up to 60 seconds, on Medium and Unlimited.
  • You don’t want a hosting clock. Paid events aren’t deleted automatically; guests can open the album for 30 days after the event date.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event in the iPhone app or at host.allshots.app. Name it, add the date and choose how many shots each guest gets. No account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it on one of the 12 card designs, put it on the tables, add it to the invitation.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. A page opens in their browser: nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live album you control: delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

Is GuestPix free?

GuestPix has a free plan with up to 50 photos, unlimited guests and a 30-day upload window; videos are on paid packages. Wedding packages start from $49, paid once (checked November 13, 2026). AllShots Starter is free for up to 10 guests and 50 photos, kept 7 days after the first upload.

Does AllShots keep original full-resolution photos like GuestPix?

No. AllShots resizes photos on the phone before upload, to 1920 px wide in the app and 1600 px on the long edge in the browser. GuestPix says its ZIP download has photos and videos in their original full resolution.

Which is cheaper for a small party?

For up to 10 guests, AllShots is free (50 photos, kept 7 days), and $3.99 covers up to 20 guests with unlimited photos and no automatic deletion. GuestPix’s free plan has unlimited guests but holds up to 50 photos, and its wedding packages start from $49.
