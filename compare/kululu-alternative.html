Kululu Alternative: AllShots vs Kululu (2026)

Kululu puts a Live Photo Wall on every plan and lets unlimited participants join. AllShots is a live shared album with one-time prices in dollars, from free to $29.99, and no automatic deletion on paid plans. Here is how they compare, using Kululu’s own pages.

By the AllShots team · Updated November 2026

AllShots is our app. Everything about Kululu on this page comes from its home page, pricing page and help center, checked on November 13, 2026. Kululu’s plan prices load by script inside its pricing page, so we don’t quote them; that day the page also showed a banner with 50% off all plans for a limited time.

The short answer

  • Choose Kululu if you want photos on a TV or projector during the event, unlimited participants on every plan, original quality downloads, text posts and captions, or approval of uploads before they appear (its pricing page points to the Pro plan for moderation tools).
  • Choose AllShots if you want one-time prices from free to $29.99 or an album that isn’t deleted automatically on paid plans. AllShots also has disposable mode and voice messages from guests, which the Kululu pages we checked don’t mention.

When Kululu is the better pick

  • You want the photos on a screen. The Live Photo Wall comes with every plan.
  • You expect a big crowd on a small budget. Every plan, Free included, supports unlimited participants; the limits are on uploads and time.
  • You need originals. Plus and Pro download in original quality.
  • You run a public event. Turn on moderation and approve every upload before it appears; Kululu’s pricing page points to its Pro plan for this.
  • Your event runs over several days. Kululu describes Plus as ideal for mid-size or multi-day events.

When AllShots is the better pick

  • You don’t want an album that runs out. Paid events aren’t deleted automatically, while Kululu’s access ends after 90 days on Plus and a year on Pro.
  • You like the disposable-camera idea. Each guest gets a set number of shots, with no preview or retakes in the iPhone app. The Kululu pages we checked don’t mention a set number of shots per guest.
  • You want voice messages. On Medium and Unlimited, each guest can leave a voice message of up to 60 seconds as well as a written note. Kululu’s pages describe text posts and captions from guests, and don’t mention voice messages.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event in the iPhone app or at host.allshots.app. Name it, add the date and choose how many shots each guest gets. No account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it on one of the 12 card designs, put it on the tables, drop the link in the group chat.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. A page opens in their browser: nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live album you control: delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

How long does Kululu keep photos?

Kululu’s help center says photos and videos stay accessible for 7 days on Free, 90 days on Plus and 1 year on Pro, and after that they’re no longer accessible (checked November 13, 2026). On AllShots, Starter photos are deleted 7 days after the first upload, and paid events aren’t deleted automatically.

Does AllShots have a live photo wall like Kululu?

No. AllShots plans don’t include a TV slideshow; the album updates live on guests’ phones. If a wall on a TV or projector matters, Kululu includes its Live Photo Wall on all plans.

How much does Kululu cost?

Kululu’s plans are Free, Plus and Pro, each a one-time payment per event; the prices load inside its pricing page, so check kululu.com/pricing for current amounts. AllShots costs $3.99, $14.99 or $29.99 per event, and Starter is free.
