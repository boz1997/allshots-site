Wedibox Alternative: AllShots vs Wedibox (2026)

Wedibox is a wedding hub: guest photos plus RSVP, a seating chart, a wedding website and an audio guestbook, for $29 to $79 one time. AllShots does one job, the shared photo album, from free to $29.99 per event. Here is the comparison, from Wedibox’s own pages.

By the AllShots team · Updated November 2026

AllShots is our app. Everything about Wedibox on this page comes from its pricing page and home page, checked on November 13, 2026. That day its pricing page also showed a holiday sale of 25% off with a code, ending December 31; the prices below are the regular ones it lists.

The short answer

  • Choose Wedibox if you want RSVP, seating, a wedding website and the photos behind one QR code, a gallery inside a wedding website you already have, original quality downloads, a live slideshow, or storage for a year after the event (two on All-In-One).
  • Choose AllShots if you only need the photo album and want to pay less: free for up to 10 guests, $3.99 for up to 20, $14.99 for up to 50 and $29.99 for any number, with disposable mode and notes and voice messages from guests.

When Wedibox is the better pick

  • You want the wedding admin in one place. All-In-One ($79) puts RSVP with meals and plus-ones, a live seating chart, a wedding website and registry links behind the same QR code as the photos.
  • You already built a wedding website. Embed ($29) drops a gallery widget into it; next to Embed, Wedibox lists Wix, Squarespace, WedSites, Canva, The Knot, Zola, WithJoy, Minted and SayIDo.
  • You want originals and a slideshow. The Wedding plan ($49) includes an original quality ZIP and a live slideshow.
  • You want storage you can plan around. A year after the event on every paid plan, two years on All-In-One, and the upload window starts on the event day, so you can buy early.
  • You want a money back guarantee. Wedibox offers 14 days.

When AllShots is the better pick

  • You only need the photo album. $14.99 covers up to 50 guests, and $29.99 has no guest limit.
  • A small event. Free for up to 10 guests, or $3.99 for up to 20, with unlimited photos and no automatic deletion.
  • You like the disposable-camera idea. Each guest gets a set number of shots, with no preview or retakes in the iPhone app.
  • You want both written and spoken messages. On Medium and Unlimited, each guest can leave a note of up to 240 characters and a voice message of up to 60 seconds; you download the notes as a PDF and the voices as a ZIP.

One honest limit: AllShots doesn’t keep original-resolution photos and has no TV slideshow in its plans. If either matters, Wedibox’s Wedding plan has both.

How AllShots works, in one minute

  1. Create the event in the iPhone app or at host.allshots.app. Name it, add the date and choose how many shots each guest gets. No account needed to start.
  2. Share the QR code (or the 6-character join code). Print it on one of the 12 card designs, put it on the tables, add it to the invitation.
  3. Guests scan and shoot. A page opens in their browser: nothing to install, no account. iPhone users can also use the app.
  4. Everything lands in one live album you control: delete anything, and download the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions people ask

Is Wedibox a one-time payment?

Yes. Wedibox lists $29 (Embed), $49 (Wedding) and $79 (All-In-One), each paid once, plus a free plan with 50 guest photo uploads (checked November 13, 2026). AllShots is also one-time per event: free, $3.99, $14.99 or $29.99.

Can AllShots replace Wedibox’s RSVP and seating chart?

No. AllShots is the photo album: guests’ photos, plus written notes and voice messages on Medium and Unlimited and video on Unlimited. If you want RSVP, a seating chart and a wedding website next to the photos, Wedibox All-In-One covers that.

Which one keeps photos longer?

Wedibox stores every paid gallery for a year after the event, and All-In-One for two. On AllShots, paid events aren’t deleted automatically: the album stays until you delete the event, and guests can open it for 30 days after the event date. AllShots Starter keeps photos 7 days after the first upload; Wedibox’s pricing page doesn’t say how long free galleries are kept.
