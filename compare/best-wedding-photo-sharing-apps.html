Best QR Code Wedding Photo Sharing Apps (2026), Compared

Nine ways to get your guests’ photos into one place with a QR code or a link, side by side: whether guests need an app or an account, video, original files, a live slideshow, how you pay and what is free.

By the AllShots team · Updated November 2026

The short version

  • You want original, full-resolution files: GuestPix, Guestreel and WedUploader say they keep originals, Wedibox has an original quality ZIP on its Wedding plan, and Kululu downloads in original quality on paid plans. AllShots resizes photos on the phone, so it isn’t the pick here.
  • You want guest photos on a TV at the reception: Kululu has a Live Photo Wall on all plans and Lense includes one with every event; GuestPix, Guestreel, Wedibox (Wedding and All-In-One) and WedUploader (premium) have one too.
  • You want RSVP, seating and a wedding website in the same place: Wedibox All-In-One.
  • You want the files in your own Google Drive: WedUploader.
  • You want a disposable camera with a next-day reveal: POV. Lense lets you choose an instant, delayed or private album, and AllShots has a disposable mode with 10 to 72 shots per guest.
  • You have a small guest list and a small budget: AllShots is free for up to 10 guests and $3.99 for up to 20. POV is free for up to 10 guests and Lense for up to 7.
  • Your family already shares on Google Photos: a shared album can work; read the pros and cons for a wedding first.

Best for, option by option

AllShots

Best for: hosts who want every guest to join from any phone’s browser, with no app and no account, free for up to 10 guests and a low one-time price after that ($3.99 for up to 20 guests, $29.99 for unlimited guests). You also get disposable mode (10 to 72 shots per guest), written notes and voice messages on Medium and Unlimited, and a ZIP of everything on every plan.

Pick something else if you need original-resolution photos or a slideshow on a TV: we resize photos on the phone before upload, and our plans don’t include a TV wall.

Sources: our pricing page and FAQ.

GuestPix

Best for: couples who want a complete wedding gallery: an original full-resolution ZIP, a live slideshow, a written and video guestbook, an invitation with RSVPs, and hosting for 12 months from the event date (the Signature Bundle has 24 months of upload and hosting, starting from the date of purchase). Wedding packages start from $49, one time, with a 14-day money back guarantee.

AllShots fits better for small events and small budgets: free for up to 10 guests, $3.99 for up to 20.

Sources, checked November 13, 2026: GuestPix wedding pricing, GuestPix FAQ. More in AllShots vs GuestPix.

Wedibox

Best for: couples who want the photos plus RSVP with meals and plus-ones, a live seating chart, a wedding website and registry links behind one QR code (All-In-One, $79), or a gallery widget inside a wedding website they already have (Embed, $29). The $49 Wedding plan adds a live slideshow, an audio guestbook and an original quality ZIP, with a year of storage.

AllShots fits better if you only need the photo album and want to pay less than Wedibox’s Wedding ($49) or All-In-One ($79) plans: $14.99 for up to 50 guests, $29.99 for unlimited guests. If you already have a wedding website, Wedibox’s Embed plan is $29.

Sources, checked November 13, 2026: Wedibox pricing, Wedibox home page. More in AllShots vs Wedibox.

Kululu

Best for: events that want a Live Photo Wall on a TV or projector on every plan, with unlimited participants on every plan and original quality downloads on Plus and Pro.

Keep in mind: on Free, uploads stay open for 24 hours and photos stay accessible for 7 days; on Plus, access lasts 90 days, and on Pro, a year.

Sources, checked November 13, 2026: Kululu pricing, Kululu help: plans, Kululu help: access. More in AllShots vs Kululu.

POV

Best for: the disposable-camera ritual: the host caps photos per guest and can have photos reveal the next day. Guests join through an App Clip on iPhone or the web on Android, it’s free for up to 10 guests, and the app is rated 4.9 from 16K ratings on the US App Store.

AllShots fits better for big guest lists: Unlimited is $29.99 for any number of guests, and POV’s in-app purchases go up to $89.99. Our Unlimited plan also includes video clips up to 90 seconds; the POV pages we checked don’t say whether POV supports video.

Sources, checked November 13, 2026: POV on the App Store, POV home page, POV pricing. More in AllShots vs POV.

Lense

Best for: disposable-style events that want a Live Photo Wall included with every event, an instant, delayed or private album, 12 months of storage and capture that keeps working offline after a guest joins. Free for up to 7 guests with 25 captures each; on the US App Store, 25 guests cost $4.99.

Sources, checked November 13, 2026: Lense home page, Lense FAQ, Lense pricing, Lense llms.txt, Lense on the App Store.

WedUploader

Best for: hosts who want every original file delivered into their own Google Drive, with no expiration date and one premium payment that covers all their albums. You sign up and connect Google Drive, and storage is bounded by your Drive space; their FAQ points to the 15 GB Google gives for free.

Sources, checked November 13, 2026: WedUploader home page, WedUploader pricing, WedUploader FAQ.

Guestreel

Best for: hosts who want originals at full resolution, a live Picture Wall and simple one-time prices: $39 Premium, $69 Unlimited, and moving from Premium to Unlimited costs only the difference. The free plan is a short test run (3 guests, 10 photos and videos, 7 days), and Premium keeps photos for 30 days; Unlimited keeps them for a year.

Sources, checked November 13, 2026: Guestreel pricing, Guestreel home page.

Google Photos shared album

Best for: families already on Google Photos who want the wedding album next to the rest of their photos. You can share it by link or QR code, and people with the link can view the album and add to it.

Keep in mind: when someone leaves a shared album, the photos and videos they added are removed, and if you stop sharing, photos other people added are removed too.

Sources, checked November 13, 2026: Google Photos Help: share, Google Photos Help: leave an album, Google Photos Help: stop sharing. More in Google Photos for a wedding.

Questions people ask

Do wedding guests need to download an app to share photos?

With most options here, no. AllShots, GuestPix, Wedibox, Kululu, Lense, WedUploader and Guestreel work in the phone’s browser. POV uses an App Clip on iPhone and a web experience on Android. Google says you can share a Google Photos album even with people who don’t use the Google Photos app.

Which options keep original, full-resolution files?

GuestPix, Guestreel and WedUploader say they keep originals, Wedibox includes an original quality ZIP on its Wedding plan, and Kululu downloads in original quality on its paid plans. AllShots doesn’t: photos are resized on the phone before upload, to 1920 px wide in the app and 1600 px on the long edge in the browser.

Which ones can show guest photos on a TV during the reception?

Kululu (all plans), Lense (every event), GuestPix, Guestreel, Wedibox (Wedding and All-In-One) and WedUploader (premium) describe a live slideshow or photo wall, and POV lists live slideshows with its paid business plans. AllShots plans don’t include a TV slideshow; the album updates live on guests’ phones.

Is there a free QR code wedding photo app?

Most have a free tier with limits: AllShots (10 guests, 50 photos, kept 7 days), POV (up to 10 guests), Lense (up to 7 guests), GuestPix (50 photos), Wedibox (50 photo uploads), Kululu (100 uploads, 24 hours to upload) and Guestreel (3 guests, 10 photos and videos, 7 days). WedUploader gives every album a QR code, link and printable signs for free, and for Google Photos, Google’s help pages describe storage (up to 15 GB with each Google Account) rather than a price per event.
